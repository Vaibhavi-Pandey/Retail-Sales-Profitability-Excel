# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis (Excel Dashboard)

## Overview
An interactive Microsoft Excel sales and profitability dashboard analyzing 9,994 e-commerce transaction records across 22 fields to track sales, profit, profit margin, product category performance, regional/state sales, and customer/product trends.

## Repository Contents
- `Retail_Sales_Profitability_Excel_Dashboard.xlsx` – Excel workbook containing raw data (`Data`), prepared data (`Working_Data`), pivot and summary tables (`Pivot_Table`, `KPI`), and the dashboard (`Final_Dashboard`).
- `Retail_Sales_Profitability_Excel_Dashboard.pdf` – Exported visual snapshot/report of the dashboard.

## Tools & Features Used
-  Microsoft Excel:  Data preparation, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Conditional Formatting, and KPI cards.
-  Visualizations:  Sales & profit trend chart, category profit comparison, category sales share chart, state-wise sales map, top customers chart, and top products by quantity.

## Key Metrics & Insights
-  Headline Metrics:  Tracked  $2.30M  (`$2,297.20K`) in total sales,  $286.40K  in total profit ( 12.47%  profit margin),  37,873  total quantity, and  9,994  transaction records.
-  Category & Regional Highlights:   Technology  leads profit (~$145.45K, ~36.40% of sales share) while  Furniture  has the lowest profit (~$18.45K, ~32.30% of sales share) and  Office Supplies  accounts for ~31.30% of sales share (~$122.49K profit), with  California  and  New York  among top-performing states.
